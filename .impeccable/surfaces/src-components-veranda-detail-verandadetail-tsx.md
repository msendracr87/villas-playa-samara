## Scope and mode

Veranda Sport Bar detail route at `/dining/veranda`. Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience, job, and action

Prospective resort guests understand Veranda as an informal, social sport-bar concept for drinks, shareable snacks, sports viewing, and easygoing time with family and friends. They can explore its drinks and snacks concept menus, recognize that branding, selections, and programming remain under review, return to the dining overview, or navigate back to Trattoria.

## Proof and constraints

Use `docs/copy/dining/sportbar/sport-bar-website-copy.md` as canonical copy and the supplied Veranda photography and logo assets. Veranda's name and logo are concept branding and must remain labeled as subject to change. Mockup menu links are allowed by explicit owner direction, but their destinations must remain visibly labeled as concept material rather than current published menus. Do not expose unapproved operating hours, sports schedules, entertainment programming, reservation or seating policies, or an unconfirmed Guest Services destination. Decorative photography uses empty alt text until production alt text is approved.

## Direction and memorable moment

Extend the established dining world through a social playbook built around four clear actions: meet, watch, share, and unwind. Use night navy, warm amber, and quiet sand; pair broad scoreboard-like information bands with the supplied sport-bar photography and a decisive drinks-versus-snacks menu passage. The hero inherits the venue-page convention with its section journey cue at lower-left and outlined “All dining” action at lower-right. The memorable moment is a wide social-play band that reads as a calm resort interpretation of game-night information rather than a literal sports broadcast interface.
