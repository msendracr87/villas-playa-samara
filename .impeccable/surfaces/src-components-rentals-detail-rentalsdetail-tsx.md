## Scope and mode

Rentals detail route. Visitor mode: Experience.

## Audience, job, and action

Prospective guests compare the character, inclusions, departure information, and prices of the ATV Jungle Tour, Fishing Trip, and Bike Rental, then submit the details needed for an inquiry. The primary job is understanding which way of exploring fits their pace; the secondary path continues into Day Tours through the Experiences overview until its dedicated route is approved.

## Proof and constraints

Use `docs/copy/experiences/rentals/rentals-copy.md` as the canonical content source and `assets/images/experiences/rentals/` as the visual source. The project owner explicitly approved publishing the supplied Rentals prices and departure information on 2026-08-11. Do not invent contact destinations or alt text.

## Direction and memorable moment

Extend the approved Experiences visual world through a composed field-guide sequence. An immersive ATV hero and centered introduction open into three large rental folios, each combining its sharp photograph with the matching supplied blurred landscape. Alternating image placement gives the sequence rhythm, equal-width image columns keep it consistent, and practical operational details remain easy to scan. Each folio provides a direct inquiry action that carries its rental selection into the form, moves the guest to that form, and places focus on the selected-rental control. The journey closes with the established Rooms & Villas inquiry composition and textured forest background, adapted to rental selection, participants, date, and time.

## Responsive behavior

Desktop folios alternate image placement within a two-column composition. Tablet and mobile collapse to image-first single-column stories while preserving source order, readable copy, and the page’s navigation path. Decorative images remain empty-alt pending production alt-text approval, and motion is removed for reduced-motion users.
