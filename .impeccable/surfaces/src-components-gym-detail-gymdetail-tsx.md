# GYM detail surface

- **Scope and mode:** `/wellness/gym` current GYM detail page; Persuade.
- **Audience and job:** Prospective and current guests need to understand the current GYM setting, equipment, and access, then ask Guest Services a question or continue into the Spa offering.
- **Proof and content:** Owner-supplied GYM photographs and canonical GYM copy.
- **Constraints:** Keep current facts aligned with canonical copy. Do not invent operating policies, contact details, required form fields, or a submission destination.
- **Direction:** Extend the established Wellness detail-page system. The inquiry section uses the shared textured forest-and-sand form treatment, followed by a compact photographic cross-link band that previews the destination Spa experience.
- **Primary actions:** Review the GYM, send a future Guest Services inquiry once delivery is approved, explore Spa, or return to Wellness.
- **Unresolved decisions:** Form delivery route, required fields, privacy and consent handling, success and error states, and approved production alt text.
