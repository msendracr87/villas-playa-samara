## Scope and mode

Arrecife restaurant detail route at `/dining/arrecife`. Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience, job, and action

Prospective resort guests understand Arrecife as the property's main restaurant and bar, learn how its seasonal service can move between buffet and à la carte formats, and decide whether it fits their stay. The primary available action is returning to the complete dining collection; reservation assistance is omitted until an approved destination exists.

## Proof and constraints

Use `docs/copy/dining/arrecife/arrecife-restaurant-website-copy.md` as canonical copy and the supplied Arrecife photography and logo assets. Arrecife's name and logo are concept branding and must remain labeled as subject to change. Mockup menu links are allowed by explicit owner direction, but their destinations must remain visibly labeled as concept material rather than current published menus. Do not expose unapproved operating hours or an unconfirmed reservation destination. Decorative photography uses empty alt text until production alt text is approved.

## Direction and memorable moment

Extend the established dining world through an all-day restaurant journey: an immersive photographic cover led by the white Arrecife concept logo, an editorial welcome, seasonal buffet and à la carte storytelling, a dark integrated-bar passage, an asymmetric atmosphere gallery, and a composed visit-planning close. The hero follows the venue-page action convention with the section journey cue at lower-left and an outlined “All dining” action at lower-right. The memorable shift is the page moving from bright open-air dining into the deep-green bar section before returning to daylight.
