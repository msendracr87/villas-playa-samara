## Scope and mode

Dining overview route. Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience, job, and action

Prospective guests understand the resort's four dining venues, compare their service occasions, and distinguish Nikoa Beach Club from the all-inclusive dining collection. The primary action is exploring the four resort venues; secondary actions are visiting Nikoa's documented official website and requesting dining assistance once an approved Guest Services destination is provided.

## Proof and constraints

Use `docs/copy/dining/dining-overview-website-copy.md` as canonical copy. Preserve the four-venue scope and order; present Arrecife, Baja Azul, Trattoria, and Veranda—and their supplied logos—as concept branding that may change before final approval. Omit unapproved hours and menus, and present Nikoa as a separate additional-charge venue. Use only approved dining imagery and supplied logo assets.

## Direction and memorable moment

The established resort world extends into a food-led page: an immersive photographic hero, a centered introduction with a three-image complementary-food collage, and four fixed-height restaurant stories stacked with alternating image and copy placement. Each restaurant story uses its concept logo as the primary venue heading and follows it with a concise concept-status label. Nikoa uses the same single-image, two-column rhythm but lets its image bleed to the viewport's left edge; its official logo replaces the text heading within the navy copy panel. The page closes with a centered, image-free dining-assistance outro that mirrors the introduction's composure.
