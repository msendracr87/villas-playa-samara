# Gallery page

## Scope and mode

- Primary surface: `/gallery` in Experience mode.
- The page extends the established VPS visual system rather than creating a separate identity.

## Visitor job and content

- Help prospective guests explore the resort through the approved 35-image Gallery collection.
- Use the canonical homepage Gallery introduction until dedicated Gallery copy is approved.
- Do not add filtering until categories and per-image assignments are approved.
- Keep production captions and image alt text pending rather than inventing them.

## Chosen direction

- A living resort mosaic: a full-viewport scenic opening followed by a variable-height image wall.
- Shuffle the collection on each fresh page load; keep Gallery image 35 as the fixed scenic hero.
- The memorable interaction is a restrained full-screen viewer with keyboard, touch, and focus support.

## Unresolved decisions

- Gallery categories and filter labels.
- Per-image category assignments.
- Final captions, alt text, image provenance, rights, and drone-image publication approval.
