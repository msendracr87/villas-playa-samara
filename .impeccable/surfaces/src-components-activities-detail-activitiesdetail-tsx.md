## Scope and mode

Activities detail route. Visitor mode: Experience.

## Audience, job, and action

Prospective guests compare five Sámara activities by setting, inclusions, departure conditions, and review-stage price before submitting an inquiry. The primary job is choosing an activity that fits the guest’s pace and interests; the secondary path returns to Day Tours or continues into Rentals.

## Proof and constraints

Use `docs/copy/experiences/activities/activities-copy.md` as the canonical content source and `assets/images/experiences/activities/` as the visual source. Do not publish the unconfirmed padel and pickleball schedule or equipment availability. Inquiry delivery remains visibly unconnected until its production destination is approved.

## Direction and memorable moment

Extend the approved Experiences field-guide system into five continuous chapters spanning courts, Isla Chora, surf, and wildlife. Each sharp activity photograph sits over its matching blurred companion, practical details remain easy to scan, and every chapter carries its selection directly into the inquiry form. The page closes the Experiences-detail loop by leading guests toward Rentals, as directed by the canonical copy.

## Responsive behavior

Desktop chapters use equal-width image and copy columns and alternate image placement. Tablet and mobile collapse into image-first stories while preserving source order. The five-item index shifts from three columns to two and then one, the inquiry form becomes one column, and reduced-motion users receive the complete static composition.

## Unresolved decisions

Public display approval and last-reviewed dates for prices, departure conditions, and inclusions remain pending. Padel and pickleball schedules, equipment availability, reservation requirements, and guest access remain tracked in `TO-DO.md`. Inquiry routing, production alt text, image approvals, ownership, and usage rights also remain pending.
