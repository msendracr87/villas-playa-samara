# Morpho Spa detail surface

- **Scope and mode:** `/wellness/spa` current-services detail page; Persuade.
- **Audience and job:** Prospective and current guests need to understand what Morpho Spa offers now, compare treatments and packages, and learn how to arrange an appointment without confusing current services with the future retreat.
- **Proof and content:** The owner-confirmed Morpho treatment menu, dedicated Spa location, approved Morpho logo, and two owner-selected current-treatment photographs.
- **Constraints:** Present only current services. Do not show or describe future retreat facilities. Keep treatment names, durations, prices, and package inclusions aligned with the canonical menu. Do not invent operating hours, appointment policies, contact details, or a booking destination.
- **Direction:** Extend the established Wellness detail-page system. A treatment-led photographic hero introduces Morpho, a quiet image-and-copy chapter establishes the current setting, and a light sand full-width menu chapter makes comparison the central page experience without card decoration. The page closes with the shared dark inquiry treatment and a compact cross-link that previews the destination GYM experience.
- **Primary actions:** Move to the treatment menu, submit a future Guest Services inquiry once delivery is approved, explore the GYM, or return to Wellness.
- **Unresolved decisions:** Final Guest Services destination, Spa operating hours, appointment policies, required form fields, privacy and consent handling, success and error states, and approved production alt text for both photographs.
