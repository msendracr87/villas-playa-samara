# Morpho Spa detail surface

- **Scope and mode:** `/wellness/spa` current-services detail page; Persuade.
- **Audience and job:** Prospective and current guests need to understand what Morpho Spa offers now, compare treatments and packages, and learn how to arrange an appointment without confusing current services with the future retreat.
- **Proof and content:** The owner-confirmed Morpho treatment menu, dedicated Spa location, approved Morpho logo, and two owner-selected current-treatment photographs.
- **Constraints:** Present only current services. Do not show or describe future retreat facilities. Keep treatment names, durations, prices, and package inclusions aligned with the canonical menu. Do not invent operating hours, appointment policies, contact details, or a booking destination.
- **Direction:** Extend the established Wellness detail-page system. A treatment-led photographic hero introduces Morpho, a quiet image-and-copy chapter establishes the current setting, and a dark full-width menu chapter makes comparison the central page experience without card decoration.
- **Primary actions:** Move to the treatment menu, return to the Wellness overview, and contact Guest Services during the stay using confirmed resort channels.
- **Unresolved decisions:** Final Guest Services destination, Spa operating hours, appointment policies, and approved production alt text for both photographs.
