## Scope and mode

Rooms & Villas overview and shared accommodation-detail template. Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience, job, and action

Prospective couples, families, friends, and groups compare the eight approved accommodation categories by sleeping capacity, bed configuration, setting, outdoor space, and summary features. The primary action is opening one accommodation; secondary wayfinding moves between adjacent stays or back to the full collection.

## Proof and constraints

Use only the canonical names, order, occupancy, summaries, and amenities from `docs/copy/accommodations/`. Use active approved-path imagery and never the accommodation backup directory. Omit pending sizes, floor plans, booking URLs, contact destinations, and unapproved production alt text. Preserve the established homepage design system.

## Direction and memorable moment

A numbered editorial catalog begins with a full-height beachfront image, then alternates quiet context with a staggered two-column accommodation index. Detail pages turn the same data into a full-bleed introduction, comparison strip, approved narrative, feature list, and previous/next route.

## Unresolved decisions

Room-specific booking destinations, final production alt text, sizes, floor plans, and the Junior Suite and Two Bedroom Villa configuration confirmations remain in `TO-DO.md`.
