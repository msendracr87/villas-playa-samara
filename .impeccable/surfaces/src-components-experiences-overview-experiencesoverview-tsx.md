## Scope and mode

Experiences overview route. Visitor mode: Experience.

## Audience, job, and action

Prospective guests explore the range and pace of adventures available through Sámara, Monkey Tours, and the resort's in-house activity program. The primary action is understanding whether Rentals, Day Tours, or Activities fits their trip; secondary actions are browsing the in-house activity collection and requesting planning help once an approved Guest Services destination is provided.

## Proof and constraints

## Direction and memorable moment

The established resort world expands into a two-tempo page: an immersive Pura Vida hero and composed Monkey Tours introduction lead into a dark, sticky journey sequence, then release into a bright horizontal gallery of in-house activities. The journey sequence mirrors the homepage Experiences showcase through a centered story that progressively fades while three large category panels pass over it, but uses alternating editorial panels instead of repeating the homepage's three-column cards. The in-house carousel becomes the tactile counterpoint, with square image geometry, visible position feedback, and one-to-three cards shown according to the viewport.
