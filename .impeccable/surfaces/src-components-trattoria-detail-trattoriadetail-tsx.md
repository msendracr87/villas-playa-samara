## Scope and mode

Trattoria restaurant detail route at `/dining/trattoria`. Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience, job, and action

Prospective resort guests understand Trattoria as a relaxed, polished Italian-inspired dinner venue. They can explore its à la carte, drinks, and wine concept menus, recognize that the branding and selections remain under review, return to the dining overview, or navigate back to Baja Azul.

## Proof and constraints

Use `docs/copy/dining/italian-restaurant/italian-restaurant-website-copy.md` as canonical copy and the supplied Trattoria photography and logo assets. Trattoria's name and logo are concept branding and must remain labeled as subject to change. Mockup menu links are allowed by explicit owner direction, but their destinations must remain visibly labeled as concept material rather than current published menus. Do not expose unapproved operating hours, reservation requirements, or an unconfirmed Guest Services destination. Decorative photography uses empty alt text until production alt text is approved.

## Direction and memorable moment

Extend the established dining world through an Italian evening in three meaningful acts: arrival and aperitivo, familiar food shared at the table, then wine and a slower close. Use warm ivory, restrained terracotta, and deep wine tones; let the supplied restaurant render establish the first viewport, use portrait food photography like editorial plates, and give the wine passage a darker, intimate change of pace. The hero establishes the shared venue-page action convention with the section journey cue at lower-left and an outlined “All dining” action at lower-right; Veranda Sport Bar inherits it. The signature structure is the evening advancing through clearly named acts rather than repeating Arrecife's all-day service or Baja Azul's day-to-night split.
