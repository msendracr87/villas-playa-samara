# Day Tours

**Discover Costa Rica in a Day**

### Adventures Beyond the Shores of Sámara

Step into a world of natural wonders with our curated day tours. From volcanic landscapes and cloud forests to coffee plantations and wildlife reserves, each tour is designed to bring you closer to Costa Rica's vibrant culture and breathtaking beauty — all within a day's journey from Sámara.

---

## Sunset Ocean Tour

[Sunset Ocean Tour](../../../../assets/images/experiences/daytours/sunset-tour.jpg)

Sail along the coast of Sámara to spot dolphins and, during migration season, humpback whales. Afterwards, snorkel to discover vibrant underwater life.

**Includes:**
- Fruits
- Fresh water
- Beer

**Departure:**
3:30 p.m. (subject to availability)

**Price:**
Starting at: $69 p.p.

## Palo Verde Boat Tour & Pottery Town

[Palo Verde Boat Tour & Pottery Town](../../../../assets/images/experiences/daytours/palo-verde-tour.jpg)
