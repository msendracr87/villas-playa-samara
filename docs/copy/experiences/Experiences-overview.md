# Experiences Overview

[Hero Image](../../../assets/images/experiences/activities/padel-and-pickleball.jpg)

**Experience the Pura Vida**

### Your Gateway to Adventure

Explore Costa Rica's wild beauty.

Embark on a journey of discovery with Monkey Tours, a standalone tour operator based at Villas Playa Sámara and serving destinations throughout Costa Rica and the Sámara area. Expert guides bring the country's landscapes, culture, and wildlife to life through tours designed for different interests and travel styles.

Questions and tour inquiries are handled through the Villas Playa Sámara website so guests can plan an experience as part of their stay.

> Tour and activity schedules are subject to availability. Departure times for selected experiences may vary according to tide conditions. Contact Guest Services for current details during your stay.
