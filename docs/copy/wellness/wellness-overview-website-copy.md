## Yoga

**Section label:** Guided Yoga

# Reconnect, breathe, and flow

Guided yoga offers a gentle way to reconnect with your breath, body, and sense of balance during your stay.

Each session creates time for mindful movement in relaxed resort settings connected to the natural surroundings of Sámara. Free group sessions are available on selected mornings and afternoons, while private sessions can be arranged for a more personal practice.

### Free Yoga Sessions

**Maximum capacity:** 15 participants

| Day | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | 4:30 p.m. |
| Tuesday | 4:30 p.m. |
| Thursday | 8:00 a.m. |

### Session Locations

- Morning classes are held on the beach in front of Nikoa Beach Club.
- Afternoon classes are held above Arrecife Restaurant.
- In case of bad weather, sessions are held in an air-conditioned room above Arrecife Restaurant.

### Private Yoga Sessions

Private sessions are also available. Contact Guest Services to arrange your practice.

## Morpho Future Development

**Section label:** Under Development

# A more expansive retreat is taking shape

Morpho Wellness Retreat is being reimagined as a more complete, nature-connected wellness destination within Villas Playa Sámara.

Building on the treatments available today, the future retreat is planned to introduce additional treatment spaces, a sauna, cold plunge, retreat pool, beauty areas, and more places for quiet rest among tropical surroundings.

The expanded experience is envisioned as a calm sequence of treatment, water, recovery, beauty, and relaxation spaces that guests can enjoy at an unhurried pace.

### Planned Experiences

- Expanded massage and treatment spaces
- Sauna and cold plunge
- Retreat pool
- Landscaped relaxation areas
- Beauty Bar
- Hair Studio
- Nail Bar
- Broader body-care experiences

> These additions are future experiences and are not yet available. Images are conceptual, and the design, services, policies, and opening information may evolve as development continues.
