# Brief Description

## A more complete approach to wellbeing

The future Morpho Wellness Retreat is envisioned as a calm, nature-connected destination where wellness can unfold at an unhurried pace.

The expanded concept is planned to introduce more treatment spaces alongside a cold plunge, sauna, pool, Beauty Bar, Hair Studio, Nail Bar, and additional body-care experiences.

The architectural direction places individual treatment areas, water features, wooden walkways, and shaded relaxation zones within lush tropical landscaping. The result is designed to feel private, restorative, and closely connected to the natural character of Sámara.

# The Future Guest Journey

**Section label:** Your Morpho Wellness Retreat Experience

# From arrival to a slower rhythm

The future Morpho Wellness Retreat is envisioned as a sequence rather than a single treatment room.

Guests may begin at reception, move through landscaped paths toward a private treatment space, spend time in the sauna or cold plunge, relax beside the pool, or continue to the Beauty Bar, Hair Studio, or Nail Bar.

This more complete layout is intended to make the retreat feel like a destination within the resort—one that guests can experience for a single service or for a longer period of rest and care.

# Architecture & Atmosphere

**Section label:** Designed Around Nature

# A wellness setting shaped by the landscape

The concept places the retreat within dense tropical planting, using natural textures, warm wood, stone pathways, shaded structures, and water as central design elements.

The render suggests:

- Individual treatment pavilions
- Multiple massage rooms
- A central pool area
- Separate water and recovery zones
- Wooden walkways and decks
- Shaded relaxation areas
- A reception and service building
- Private beauty and treatment spaces
- Strong visual separation between active and quiet areas

The overall direction feels calm, private, and immersive without losing the welcoming character of Villas Playa Sámara.
