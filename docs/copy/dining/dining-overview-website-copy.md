## Dining Introduction

**Section label:** Taste Your Stay

# Gather around the table

Every day brings a new opportunity to enjoy good food and easygoing resort hospitality.

Begin with breakfast at Arrecife, stop for a casual bite between the beach and pool, or settle in for an evening of familiar Italian favorites, Mexican-inspired flavors, cocktails, and conversation.

Our restaurants and bars offer a range of settings and menus for couples, families, and friends—from relaxed all-day dining to lively social evenings. Wherever you choose, the atmosphere is welcoming and the experience is designed to fit naturally into your stay.

> Restaurant schedules, menus, and service formats may vary according to seasonality and availability. Contact Guest Services for the current dining program during your stay.

## Additional Beachfront Dining

**Section label:** Beyond the All-Inclusive Plan

# Nikoa Beach Club

Nikoa Beach Club is a separate beachfront dining experience between Villas Playa Sámara and Azura Beach Resort, offering lunch, dinner, drinks, and Pacific views.

Nikoa is not one of the four Villas Playa Sámara resort venues and is not included in the all-inclusive plan. Menu items and additional services are charged separately.

**Service:** Lunch and dinner  
**Plan:** Additional charge  
**CTA:** [Explore Nikoa Beach Club](nikoa-beach-club/nikoa-beach-club-standalone-page-and-dining-card-copy.md)
