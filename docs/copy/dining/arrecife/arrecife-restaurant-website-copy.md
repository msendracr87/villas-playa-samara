# Arrecife

## International variety, Costa Rican character, and relaxed resort dining

Arrecife is the main restaurant and bar at Villas Playa Sámara, offering a welcoming dining experience from breakfast through dinner.

Depending on the season, guests can enjoy generous buffet service or a relaxed à la carte menu featuring international, Mediterranean, Italian-inspired, Costa Rican, and contemporary flavors.

**Primary CTA:** View Menus  
**Secondary CTA:** Reservation Assistance

---

## Brief Restaurant Description

# A welcoming table for every part of the day

Arrecife brings familiar favorites, local flavors, and international variety together in one comfortable setting.

During high season, the restaurant serves buffet breakfast, lunch, and dinner. During low season, service may shift to an à la carte format, offering a more composed selection while preserving the same approachable atmosphere.

Whether joining us for a Costa Rican breakfast, a relaxed lunch, or dinner with family and friends, Arrecife is designed to offer something for different tastes and ways of dining.

## Menus

**Section label:** Food & Drinks

# Explore our menus

Arrecife’s service format and culinary selections may vary according to the season. Please review the available menus or contact Guest Services for the current dining format.

### Buffet

A varied buffet experience with familiar international dishes and Costa Rican favorites.

Breakfast may include American-style classics such as waffles, hash browns, eggs, and bacon, alongside traditional options including gallo pinto, eggs, picadillo, fresh fruit, and local accompaniments.

Lunch and dinner selections rotate between international, Mediterranean, Italian-inspired, Costa Rican, and contemporary dishes.

**CTA:** View Buffet Menu

---

### À La Carte

Available during selected periods, Arrecife’s à la carte menu offers a relaxed selection of international dishes with Mediterranean, Italian-inspired, Costa Rican, and contemporary influences.

The menu is designed to appeal to a broad range of guests while complementing the resort’s themed dining experiences.

**CTA:** View À La Carte Menu

Enjoy a selection of cocktails, wines, beers, non-alcoholic beverages, juices, coffee, and refreshments from the bar.

Availability may vary.

## Dress Code

**Section label:** What to Wear

# Casual resort attire

Casual resort attire is requested.

For the comfort of all guests, shirts or appropriate cover-ups and footwear are required. Bathing suits worn without a cover-up and shirtless entry are not permitted inside the restaurant.

Thank you for your understanding.
