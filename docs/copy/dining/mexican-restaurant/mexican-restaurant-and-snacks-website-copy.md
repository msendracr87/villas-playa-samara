## Brief Restaurant Description

# From relaxed snacks to flavorful evenings

Baja Azul brings together two complementary experiences in one casual setting.

During the day, guests can stop by for convenient snacks, light meals, and refreshments between the beach, pool, and resort activities.

In the evening, the restaurant shifts toward classic Mexican and Tex-Mex flavors, with familiar dishes designed to be shared and enjoyed with family and friends.

Expect a lively but comfortable atmosphere, approachable food, and a menu with something for different tastes.

### À La Carte Dinner Menu

The evening menu features classic Mexican and Tex-Mex-inspired dishes in a relaxed à la carte format.

Selections may include tacos, quesadillas, nachos, fajitas, burritos, grilled meats, fresh salsas, guacamole, rice, beans, and other familiar favorites.

The menu is designed to offer bold flavor while remaining approachable for families, couples, and groups.

## Dress Code

**Section label:** What to Wear

# Casual resort attire

Casual resort attire is requested.

For the comfort of all guests, shirts or appropriate cover-ups and footwear are required. Bathing suits worn without a cover-up and shirtless entry are not permitted inside the restaurant.

Thank you for your understanding.
