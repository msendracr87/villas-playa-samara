# Snacks Made for Sharing

A casual selection of familiar bar snacks, crispy favorites, and salty treats to enjoy with a cold drink, during a game, or while spending time with family and friends.

## Nachos & Shareables

### Classic Nachos

Tortilla chips topped with melted cheese, pico de gallo, jalapeños, sour cream, and guacamole.

**Allergens:** Milk

### Loaded Nachos

Tortilla chips topped with cheese, beans, seasoned beef or chicken, pico de gallo, jalapeños, sour cream, and guacamole.

**Allergens:** Milk

### Quesadilla Bites

Flour tortilla filled with melted cheese and served with pico de gallo and sour cream.

**Optional:** Chicken, beef, or vegetables

**Allergens:** Gluten, Milk

### Chips, Salsa & Guacamole

House-made tortilla chips served with fresh salsa and guacamole.

### Mini Sliders

Small beef sliders with cheese, pickles, onion, and house sauce.

**Allergens:** Gluten, Eggs, Milk, Sesame

Menus, ingredients, and available selections are subject to change according to seasonality and availability.
