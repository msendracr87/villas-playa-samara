# Nikoa Beach Club

## Premium dining beyond all-inclusive

Set between Azura Beach Resort and Villas Playa Sámara, Nikoa Beach Club offers a relaxed beachfront setting for lunch, dinner, drinks, and unhurried time by the Pacific.

With Sámara Beach just steps away, the experience brings together coastal views, contemporary cuisine, refreshing drinks, and an easygoing atmosphere designed for lingering over a meal.

## Brief Description

# A beachfront table in Sámara

Nikoa Beach Club is a coastal dining experience shaped by the relaxed rhythm of Sámara Bay.

The menu moves comfortably between Neapolitan-style pizzas, shareable snacks, fresh salads, ceviche, seafood, pasta, grilled specialties, and desserts. Guests can stop by for a casual lunch, settle in for dinner, or enjoy drinks as the afternoon light changes over the Pacific.

The atmosphere is polished without feeling formal, making Nikoa a natural choice for couples, families, friends, and guests looking for a dining experience beyond the resort’s all-inclusive offering.

## Experience Section

**Section label:** Beachfront Dining

# Stay for lunch, return for sunset

At Nikoa, the setting is part of the experience.

Warm sand, tropical greenery, and the sound of the ocean create a relaxed backdrop for long lunches, casual drinks, and dinner by the beach. The mood shifts naturally throughout the day—from bright and social in the afternoon to warmer and more intimate in the evening.

## Menu Section

**Section label:** Food & Drinks

# Coastal flavors, familiar favorites, and dishes made to share

Nikoa’s menu combines relaxed beach-club dining with a broad selection of international and coastal dishes.

Guests can choose from pizzas, snacks, salads, raw preparations, seafood, grilled dishes, pasta, desserts, and drinks.

### Neapolitan-Style Pizzas

A selection of classic and specialty pizzas with combinations such as:

- Margherita
- Pepperoni & Cheese
- Sámara
- Carbonara
- Cheesy Truffle
- Cheeseburger
- Spicy Pepperoni

### Snacks to Share

Easygoing selections designed for the table, including:

- Plantain chips, nachos, or yuca chips with selected dips
- Watermelon with tequila, lime, and sea salt
- Grilled pineapple
- Nikoa’s wings
- Nachos
- Hamburger and fries

### Soups, Salads & Raw

Fresh and lighter options may include:

- Gazpacho
- Caesar salad
- Avocado-yuzu tartare
- Chef’s salad
- Nikoa’s ceviche
- Tuna poke bowl
- Grilled fish tacos

### Main Courses

The main-course selection may include:

- Fish and chips
- Grilled fish
- Whole fried snapper
- Shrimp pasta
- Dry-aged ribeye with fries

### Desserts

Finish with a simple dessert such as:

- Pineapple split
- Hazelnut chocolate pizza
- Vanilla ice cream
- Fruit platter
- Sorbet
