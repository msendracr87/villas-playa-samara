## Brief Restaurant Description

# A relaxed taste of Italy

Trattoria brings classic flavors, welcoming service, and a comfortable evening atmosphere to Villas Playa Sámara.

The menu is centered on familiar Italian favorites, from freshly prepared pasta and pizza to satisfying main courses and traditional desserts.

Designed for couples, families, and groups, the experience feels polished without becoming overly formal—an inviting place to share a meal, enjoy a glass of wine, and settle into the evening.

## Dress Code

**Section label:** What to Wear

# Casual-elegant resort attire

Casual-elegant resort attire is requested.

For the comfort of all guests, shirts or appropriate cover-ups and footwear are required. Bathing suits worn without a cover-up and shirtless entry are not permitted inside the restaurant.

Thank you for your understanding.
