# Wines for the Table

A selection of approachable house wines and Italian-inspired labels chosen to accompany pasta, pizza, seafood, meats, and relaxed evening dining.

---

## House Wines

### House White Wine

A fresh, easy-drinking white wine served by the glass or bottle.

**Suggested style:** Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Grigio, or equivalent

### House Red Wine

A balanced red wine served by the glass or bottle.

**Suggested style:** Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon, Sangiovese, or equivalent

### House Rosé Wine

A light and refreshing rosé served by the glass or bottle, when available.

### House Sparkling Wine

A crisp sparkling wine served by the glass or bottle, when available.

---

## Sparkling Wines

### Prosecco

Fresh and lively, with notes of green apple, pear, and citrus.

**Pairing:** Antipasti, pizza, seafood, and light appetizers

### Sparkling Rosé

Fresh red-fruit character with a clean, lively finish.

**Pairing:** Salads, pizza, seafood, and desserts

---

## White Wines

### Pinot Grigio

Light-bodied, fresh, and crisp with citrus and green-fruit notes.

**Pairing:** Salads, seafood, light pasta, and pizza

### Sauvignon Blanc

Bright acidity with citrus and tropical-fruit character.

**Pairing:** Ceviche, fish, salads, and herb-based dishes

### Chardonnay

Medium-bodied with apple, citrus, and subtle creamy notes.

**Pairing:** Alfredo pasta, chicken, risotto, and fish

### Vermentino

Fresh and aromatic with citrus, herbs, and a light mineral finish.

**Pairing:** Seafood, antipasti, and Mediterranean dishes

---

## Rosé Wines

### Dry Rosé

Fresh and light with notes of red berries and citrus.

**Pairing:** Antipasti, salads, pizza, seafood, and light pasta dishes

---

## Red Wines

### Chianti

Medium-bodied with red-fruit character, gentle spice, and refreshing acidity.

**Pairing:** Bolognese, pizza, lasagna, and tomato-based pasta

### Sangiovese

Fresh red fruit, herbs, and balanced acidity.

**Pairing:** Pasta, pizza, chicken, and grilled vegetables

### Montepulciano d’Abruzzo

Round and approachable with dark-fruit notes and soft spice.

**Pairing:** Meat sauces, grilled beef, pizza, and aged cheeses

### Merlot

Smooth and medium-bodied with plum and soft berry notes.

**Pairing:** Chicken Milanese, beef, pasta, and pizza

### Cabernet Sauvignon

Fuller-bodied with dark fruit, structure, and a longer finish.

**Pairing:** Beef, hearty pasta, and rich sauces

### Primitivo

Rich and fruit-forward with ripe berry and warm spice notes.

**Pairing:** Pepperoni pizza, grilled meats, and tomato-based dishes

---

## Dessert Wines & After-Dinner Selections

### Moscato

Lightly sweet and aromatic with peach and floral notes.

**Pairing:** Fruit, panna cotta, and light desserts

### Sweet Dessert Wine
