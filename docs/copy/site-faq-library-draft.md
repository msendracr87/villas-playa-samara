## Global resort FAQs

### What kind of resort is Villas Playa Sámara?

Villas Playa Sámara is a welcoming, all-inclusive beachfront resort on Sámara Bay in Guanacaste, Costa Rica. Its rooms, suites, and villas are designed for couples, families, friends, and larger groups who want an easy-going stay close to the beach.

### Is Villas Playa Sámara located on the beach?

Yes. The resort is set beside Sámara Bay. Some accommodations offer beachfront or ocean views and others look toward the gardens or pool area, so guests can choose the setting that best suits their stay.

### Is Villas Playa Sámara family-friendly?

Yes. The collection includes rooms for two, two-Queen rooms and Junior Suites for up to four guests, two-bedroom villas for up to six guests, and a three-bedroom villa for up to eight guests. Resort activities, beach time, dining, and shared living spaces offer options for different ages and travel styles.

### Is the resort suitable for couples as well as groups?

Yes. Couples can choose King-bed garden or ocean-view rooms, while suites and multi-bedroom villas give families and groups more room to stay together. The accommodation pages show the bed configuration, maximum occupancy, view, floor plan, and unit size for each category.

### Does the resort offer Wi-Fi?

Wi-Fi internet access is included among the common in-room amenities across the accommodation collection. Guests who need a particular connection setup for work or another essential purpose should confirm their requirements with the resort before arrival.

### What can guests do at the resort and around Sámara?

Guests can combine beach and pool time with resort activities, padel and pickleball, wellness, and guided experiences. Monkey Tours, the independent tour operator located at the property, offers access to coastal activities, wildlife encounters, day tours, and other ways to explore Guanacaste and beyond.

## Accommodation FAQs

### What types of accommodations are available?

The collection includes eight categories: Deluxe Garden View rooms with either one King bed or two Queen beds, Garden View and Beachfront Junior Suites, two-bedroom Garden View and Beachfront Villas, a Deluxe Ocean View room, and a three-bedroom Beachfront Luxury Villa.

### What is the difference between a Deluxe room, a Junior Suite, and a villa?

Deluxe rooms provide a private sleeping area, bathroom, refreshment amenities, and a terrace or balcony. Junior Suites add a social area, dining space, full kitchen, and sofa beds. Two- and three-bedroom villas offer separate bedrooms, larger shared living spaces, full kitchens, and terraces for families or groups.

### Which accommodations have a full kitchen?

Both Junior Suite categories and all two- and three-bedroom villas include a fully equipped kitchen. Deluxe rooms include a mini-fridge, coffee maker, tableware, and utensils for drinks and light refreshments, but not a full kitchen.

### Which accommodations are best for couples?

The Deluxe Garden View with one King bed and the Deluxe Ocean View with one King bed each sleep two guests. Couples who prefer additional living space and a kitchen can also consider a Junior Suite, subject to the preferred bed configuration and availability.

### Which accommodations are designed for families or larger groups?

The Deluxe Garden View with two Queen beds and both Junior Suites sleep up to four guests. The two-bedroom villas sleep up to six guests, and the three-bedroom Beachfront Luxury Villa sleeps up to eight guests.

### What amenities are common across the accommodation collection?

Common in-room amenities include air conditioning, ceiling fans, an in-room safe, flat-screen HD TV, Wi-Fi, a mini-fridge, hair dryer, bathroom amenities, and a luggage rack. Layouts and additional features vary by category, so guests should also review the details for their selected accommodation.

### Can I see the floor plan and more photos before choosing?

Yes. Each accommodation detail page includes a Floor Plan action and a Gallery with the available images for that category.

### How do I know whether an accommodation has a garden or ocean view?

The view is identified in the name and at-a-glance details on each accommodation page. Garden View categories look toward the gardens or pool area, while Beachfront and Ocean View categories face the Pacific setting.

## Dining FAQs

### How many resort dining venues are there?

Villas Playa Sámara has four resort dining concepts: Arrecife, Baja Azul, Trattoria, and Veranda. All four names and their supplied logos are concepts for owner review and may change before final approval.

### What kind of dining is available at Arrecife?

Arrecife is the resort's main restaurant, with breakfast, lunch, and dinner service. Its program may combine buffet and à la carte service depending on the season and current operation.

### What other dining styles can guests enjoy?

The resort's dining program includes Italian-inspired dinners at Trattoria, Mexican-inspired meals and snacks at Baja Azul, and drinks and light bites at Veranda. These venue names are concepts and may change. Current service times and formats should be confirmed during the stay because they can vary.

### Is Nikoa Beach Club one of the four resort restaurants?

No. Nikoa Beach Club is a separate beachfront venue and is not one of the four resort dining venues.

### Is Nikoa Beach Club included in the all-inclusive plan?

No. Nikoa Beach Club is an additional-charge experience and is not part of the resort's all-inclusive plan.

### Are restaurant schedules and menus the same every day?

Not necessarily. Venue schedules, service formats, and menu availability may change with seasonality and resort operations. Guest Services can share the current dining program during the stay.

### Who should I contact about dietary needs or food allergies?

Share dietary needs and food-allergy questions with Guest Services before arrival and again with the restaurant team during the stay. The team can explain the current options, but guests with a serious allergy should request specific guidance rather than relying on a general menu description.

## Experiences FAQs

### What types of experiences are available?

Options may include resort activities, beach and ocean experiences, equipment rentals, day tours, wildlife encounters, cultural outings, and wellness activities. The current program varies with weather, season, availability, and operating conditions.

### What is Monkey Tours?

Monkey Tours is an independent tour operator with a dedicated presence at Villas Playa Sámara. It offers access to guided experiences beyond the resort while allowing guests to make inquiries through the Villas Playa Sámara website and team.

### Do I need to visit another website to ask about a tour?

No. Tour inquiries and lead capture remain within the Villas Playa Sámara website. The resort team and Monkey Tours can help guests review the available options.

### What kinds of coastal activities may be available?

Depending on conditions and availability, coastal experiences may include paddleboard or kayak trips toward Isla Chora, snorkeling, surfing, fishing, sunset outings, and seasonal wildlife experiences.

### Are there activities at the resort as well as off-property tours?

Yes. Guests can combine on-property activities and court sports with excursions farther around Guanacaste and Costa Rica. Padel and pickleball are court activities; paddleboards and kayaks are separate water activities.

### Are activities suitable for children or first-time participants?

Suitability depends on the specific activity, participant age, conditions, and any health or skill requirements. Guests should share who will participate when inquiring so the team can explain the appropriate options and current restrictions.

### Are tour schedules, prices, and inclusions fixed?

No. They can vary by operator, season, weather, and availability. Guests should confirm the current price, departure time, transportation, equipment, inclusions, and cancellation terms before booking.

## Wellness FAQs

### What wellness facilities and services are available now?

Current wellness offerings include the air-conditioned GYM, guided yoga in selected resort spaces, and Morpho treatments such as massages, body treatments, facials, exfoliations, and wellness packages.

### When is the GYM open?

The current GYM copy lists 24-hour access. Guests can confirm access instructions with the resort team during their stay.

### What equipment is available in the GYM?

The GYM includes cardio and strength equipment, free weights, and space for functional movement in an air-conditioned room with natural light.

### Are yoga sessions suitable for different experience levels?

Yes. Guided sessions are designed to accommodate different levels of experience. The instructor, location, and schedule may vary, so guests should review the current program with the resort team.

### Where does yoga take place?

Sessions currently take place in selected resort spaces. A dedicated yoga deck is part of the wellness development plan and should not yet be presented as an available facility.

### What Morpho treatments are currently available?

The current offering includes massages, specialty massage options, body treatments, facials, exfoliations, and wellness packages. Guests should request the current treatment menu, availability, and prices before booking.

### Are the sauna, cold plunge, beauty areas, and retreat pool open?

Not yet. These features belong to the expanded Morpho Wellness Retreat under development and must be described as future facilities until their opening is formally confirmed.
