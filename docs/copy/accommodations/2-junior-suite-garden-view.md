## Spacious Villa with Nature Views

Enjoy extra space and the charm of lush tropical surroundings. Designed for families or friends, this suite combines a comfortable bedroom with a social area where everyone can spend time together.

The standard bedroom includes a Queen bed and a Single bed. An optional King-bed bedroom configuration may be available instead; the exact replacement setup remains to be confirmed. Two sofa beds add flexible sleeping space within the suite's four-guest maximum, while the dining table and fully equipped kitchen make shared meals and longer stays more convenient. A garden-view terrace extends the suite into its tropical setting.
