## A Spacious Family Retreat

Surrounded by tropical greenery, this two-bedroom villa gives families and groups room to stay together while maintaining privacy. The master bedroom includes a King bed, air conditioning, an in-room safe, and a private bathroom. The second bedroom offers a Queen bed, a Twin bed, air conditioning, and its own private bathroom.

A shared social area, dining space, and fully equipped kitchen make it easy to gather between beach days and resort activities. Step onto the garden-view terrace for a quiet place to enjoy the tropical surroundings. A sofa-bed configuration may be used within the villa's six-guest maximum; the exact setup should be confirmed when booking.
