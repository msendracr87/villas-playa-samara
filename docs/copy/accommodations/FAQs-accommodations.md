# Accommodations FAQs

> Example content taken from another page ([Andaz](https://andazpuravida.com/))

## What room types do you recommend for families of two adults and 3 kids?

The resort offers multiple configurations for different preferences. Families of 5 are comfortable in the following room combinations:

1. King-double connecting rooms
2. Plunge pool suites connected with a double room
3. Two Bedroom Papagayo Pool Suite
4. Two Bedroom Villa
5. Two Bedroom Penthouse
6. Three Bedroom Penthouse

## How many guests can fit in a king-bedded room?

A maximum of 3 adults with 1 extra bed. An additional $75.00 charge applies for a 3rd adult in the room.

## Are your double-bedded rooms two queens or two doubles?

The double rooms have two queen beds.

## Do the suites have a sofa bed?

No, they do not. We can add an extra bed (twin) to the living room area.
