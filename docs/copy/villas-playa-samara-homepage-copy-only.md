## Dining

**Section label:** Dining at Villas Playa Sámara

# Good food, with more ways to enjoy it

From relaxed buffet meals and daytime snacks to Italian and Mexican-inspired dinners, cocktails, and casual evenings, the resort’s four dining venues bring variety to each day while keeping everything close at hand.

### Arrecife Restaurant & Bar

The resort’s main dining venue, offering seasonal buffet and à la carte service with international variety and Costa Rican character.

### Italian Restaurant

A relaxed dinner experience centered on familiar Italian flavors, including pizza, pasta, classic desserts, wines, and cocktails.

### Mexican Restaurant & Snacks

A casual venue offering daytime snacks and a Mexican and Tex-Mex-inspired à la carte experience in the evening.

### Sport Bar

A relaxed place for cocktails, cold drinks, sports, salty treats, and casual snacks from afternoon into the evening.

## Experiences

**Section label:** Experiences

# Beach days, guided adventures, and time to play

Spend the day beside Sámara Bay, join resort activities, or plan an adventure with Monkey Tours, a standalone tour operator based at the hotel and serving destinations throughout Costa Rica and the Sámara area.

Tour questions and inquiries are handled through this website so guests can plan their experience as part of their stay.

From easy pool days and court time to ATV rides, ocean tours, surf lessons, wildlife encounters, and full-day excursions, there are experiences for every pace.

### Rentals

Explore at your own rhythm with ATV jungle tours, bike rentals, and fishing trips designed for guests who want more freedom to discover Sámara and beyond.

### Day Tours

Venture farther with curated day trips to places like Arenal Volcano, Monteverde Cloud Forest, Palo Verde, coffee plantations, wildlife parks, hot springs, and scenic ocean viewpoints.

### Ocean & Beach Activities

Paddle or kayak to Isla Chora, snorkel in warm Pacific waters, take a surf lesson at Sámara Beach, join a sunset ocean tour, or experience turtle nesting at Camaronal Wildlife Refuge.

### In-House Resort Activities

Make the most of your time on property with daily activities such as yoga, aqua aerobics, beach games, tennis, pool volleyball, dance lessons, canvas time, cocktail classes, coffee tastings, tortilla making, and beach bonfires.

### Family-Friendly Play

Enjoy easy days by the pool and beach or spend time together on the resort’s padel and pickleball courts.
