# General Information

## Where is Villas Playa Sámara located?

**Confirmed**

Villas Playa Sámara is located in Playa Sámara, Guanacaste, on Costa Rica’s Pacific coast. The resort is set beside Sámara Bay, with easy access to the beach and the town of Sámara.

## Is Villas Playa Sámara beachfront?

**Confirmed**

Yes. Villas Playa Sámara is a beachfront resort located directly beside Sámara Bay.

## Is Villas Playa Sámara family-friendly?

**Confirmed**

Yes. Villas Playa Sámara welcomes families, couples, and groups, with a range of room and villa categories, all-inclusive dining, pools, beach access, activities, mini-golf, and family-oriented spaces.

## Is the resort all-inclusive?

**Confirmed**

Yes. Villas Playa Sámara offers an all-inclusive stay with meals, selected beverages, and resort experiences included according to the current plan.

> **Management review:** Confirm the exact list of inclusions, exclusions, premium beverages, specialty dining rules, and additional-charge services before publication.

## Is Nikoa Beach Club included in the all-inclusive plan?

**Confirmed**

No. Nikoa Beach Club is a separate beachfront dining experience and is not included in the Villas Playa Sámara all-inclusive plan. Menu items and additional services are charged separately.

Official website: https://www.nikoabeachclub.com/

# Weather & Destination

## What is the weather like in Sámara?

**Evergreen draft**

Sámara has a warm tropical climate throughout the year, with distinct dry and rainy seasons.

Weather can vary from day to day, particularly during the green season, so guests may wish to check the forecast close to their travel dates.

## When is the best time to visit?

**Evergreen draft**

Sámara offers different experiences throughout the year. The drier months are popular for sunny beach days, while the green season brings lush landscapes and a quieter rhythm.

The best time depends on the type of trip you are planning.
