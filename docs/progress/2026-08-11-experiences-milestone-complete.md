# Experiences milestone completed

## Milestone summary

The Experiences section now forms a complete internal journey across the dedicated overview at `/experiences` and the Rentals, Day Tours, and Activities routes. The work extends the established Villas Playa Sámara visual system while giving each offering a clear path from discovery to a preselected inquiry.

## Experiences overview

- Updated the hero to the established page height and aligned its mixed sans-serif and lime Birthstone title treatment with Rooms & Villas and Dining.
- Centered the introduction and added the supplied Monkey Tours logo in place of an additional kicker.
- Added an editorial three-image composition using supplied Experiences imagery and a centered availability and departure-condition disclaimer.
- Reworked the Rentals, Day Tours, and Activities journey sequence onto a white section with a non-sticky heading and introduction.
- Added scroll-responsive, full-width blurred backgrounds for each journey and transparent cards that reveal the corresponding sharp image on hover.
- Added restrained black overlays for text legibility, consistent image geometry, rounded card clipping, and fully clickable journey cards.
- Added a sand-backed in-house section with an accessible horizontal activity gallery, scroll snapping, controls, position feedback, keyboard focus support, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Added the Guest Services assistance close without inventing an unapproved external inquiry destination.

## Dedicated Rentals page

## Dedicated Day Tours page

- Added compact, borderless Inquire actions to all three Rentals, nine Day Tours, and five Activities offerings.

- Kept all inquiry submissions visibly disabled until a production delivery destination, privacy handling, required fields, and operational workflow are approved.
